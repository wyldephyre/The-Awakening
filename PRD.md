# Product Requirements Document: The Awakening

## 1. Game Overview
**Title:** The Awakening
**Genre:** Post-Apocalyptic / Urban Fantasy / Junk-Punk Survival RPG
**Platform:** Multiplayer PvE on premium private servers (All Out platform).
**Core Fantasy:** Players survive in a ruined world where advanced, scavenged technology ("Junk Punk") collides with strange, mystical energies ("Urban Fantasy"). 
**Core Loop:** Scavenge resources in the wilds ➔ Build and upgrade a personal outpost ➔ Craft better gear and consumables ➔ Venture into deeper, more dangerous zones ➔ Complete quests to achieve "Awakened" status.

## 2. Character & Progression
**Mad-Libs Character Creation:** Players define their identity through a sentence: *"I am a [Descriptor] [Type] who [Focus]"*.
*   **Descriptors (Bonus Stats):** Ruthless (Edge), Curious (Spark), Steadfast (Muscle), Resourceful (Balanced).
*   **Types (Classes):** Scrapper (Brawler/Tank), Builder (Defense/Crafter), Seeker (Explorer/Balanced), Weaver (Magic/Spark focus).
*   **Core Stats:** 
    *   **HP:** Health points.
    *   **Muscle:** Physical/Brute-force energy pool.
    *   **Spark:** Mystical/Tech energy pool.
    *   **Edge:** Precision/Agility energy pool.

## 3. World & Environment (Zones)
The world is radially divided into 4 difficulty zones, with enemies, resources, and harvest nodes scaling in tier.
*   **Zone 1 (Tier 1 - Current Focus Area):** The starting area. Scavenge Scrap Piles, Metal Deposits. Enemies are basic. Needs fleshed out unique enemies and a Zone 1 Boss.
*   **Zone 2 (Tier 2):** Mid-tier. Harvest Fuel Vents, Crystal Clusters, Alloy Veins. Enemies hit harder.
*   **Zone 3 (Tier 3):** Dangerous wilds. Shard Fissures, Tech Ruins, Bio Growths. Enemies are deadly.
*   **Zone 4 (Tier 4):** The deep endgame. Fragment Sites, Essence Wells. Boss-level regular enemies.

## 4. Survival & Outpost Building
Players claim a plot of land and build a grid-based base to aid their survival.
*   **Structures:**
    *   **Wall:** Defensive barrier.
    *   **Watchtower:** Increases nearby detection/safe area.
    *   **Workshop:** Enables advanced crafting / reduces costs.
    *   **Storage:** Expands player inventory capacity.
*   **Planned Additions (Zone 1 Focus):** Active defenses (turrets that shoot back) and specialized crafting stations.

## 5. Crafting & Equipment
Players gather up to 12 distinct resources (from mundane *Scrap Metal* and *Water* to mystical *Pure Nexus Essence*).
*   **Consumables:** Craft Ration Packs, Patch Kits, and Stat Boosters (Muscle Stims, Spark Cells).
*   **Equipment Slots:** Weapon, Armor, Accessory.
*   **Gear Tiers:** Crafting progression scales from crude "Scrap Blades" and "Scrap Vests" to endgame "Nexus Sabers" and "Nexus Hearts," granting massive stat bonuses.

## 6. Combat System
Real-time action combat with client-side prediction.
*   **Current Abilities:**
    *   **Melee Slash:** Directional lunge attack with cooldown.
    *   **Dodge Roll:** Directional dash that grants brief invulnerability.
*   **Planned Additions:** Class-specific abilities (e.g., Weaver magic/Spark projectile, Scrapper heavy slam).
*   **Enemy AI:** Enemies idle, patrol around their spawn, and chase/attack when the player enters their aggro radius. Visual health bars appear upon taking damage.
*   **Death & Respawn:** Dying drops the player into a 5-second respawn screen before reviving them at origin with brief invulnerability.

## 7. Quest System (The Path to Awakening)
A linear 15-quest narrative progression divided into 4 Chapters.
*   **Chapter 1 (Getting Started):** Basic kills, gathering, and crafting.
*   **Chapter 2 (Settling In):** Building the first outpost, reaching Zone 2.
*   **Chapter 3 (Into the Wilds):** Reaching Zone 3, mass crafting, killing 25 enemies.
*   **Chapter 4 (The Deep):** Reaching Zone 4, defeating 50 enemies to finally become **"Awakened"**.

## 8. Immediate Roadmap
1. **Class Abilities:** Implement unique skills for each class (Weaver magic, Scrapper slam, etc.).
2. **Zone 1 Expansion:** Add unique enemy types and a boss specifically for Zone 1.
3. **Outpost Upgrades:** Expand outpost building with active defenses and specialized stations.
